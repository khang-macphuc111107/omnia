# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **Omnia** community a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive technical community:

- Focusing critique on code, architecture, benchmarks, and verifiable evidence rather than individuals.
- Giving and gracefully accepting technical feedback with reproducible proof.
- Crediting collaborators accurately when porting or building on their ideas and fixes.
- Respecting privacy and never posting credentials, tokens, or private personal data.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing private information, secrets, or credentials without explicit permission.
- Submitting deliberately misleading benchmark claims or fabricated verification outputs.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or close issues, comments, pull requests, and discussions that violate this Code of Conduct. Reports can be sent directly to the repository owner via GitHub.
